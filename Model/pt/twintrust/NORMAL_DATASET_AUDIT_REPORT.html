5. Diagnosis & Recommended Remediation

Diagnosis: Genuine state-logic defect.

The defect stems from coupling instantaneous numerical rate derivatives with borderline statistical outliers without requiring temporal persistence:

  • Option A (Persistence Confirmation Window): Require adverse degradation signals to persist for $\ge 3$ consecutive readings before entering DEGRADING (mirroring the stateful confirmation architecture of FAN_STALL).
  • Option B (Multi-Signal Requirement): Require $\ge 2$ concurrent physical rate signals (or active rule warnings) to enter DEGRADING. Single-sensor blips remain CAUTION.
  • Option C (Severe Anomaly Gating): Prevent borderline outliers (anomaly score > $-0.15$) from gating instantaneous rate chatter into degradation.
